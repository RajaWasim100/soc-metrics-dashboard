Definitions before charts

TTD = detected_at − occurred_at. TTR = first_response_at − detected_at. TTR is a first logged response, not containment or recovery. All timestamps normalize to UTC; missing and negative intervals are excluded and reported.

Example SQL groupings

SELECT alert_type, severity, disposition, COUNT(*) AS volume
FROM alerts GROUP BY alert_type, severity, disposition;

SELECT category, outcome, COUNT(*) AS incident_count
FROM incidents GROUP BY category, outcome;

-- For operational dashboards, derive intervals only after source/timezone validation.
